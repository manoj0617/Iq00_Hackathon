# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and other Android users who need to recover meaningful storage without browsing and judging files one by one. The demo user has completed one semester, needs room for the next, and must preserve current coursework and personal camera photos.

## Product Purpose

This release is a mobile-first browser demonstration. Storage Intelligence turns supported requests into inspectable constraints, builds a deterministic plan over seeded sample records, lets users review and simulate cleanup, and verifies the resulting demo state. It does not access or delete phone files. Success means the complete workflow is interactive, consistent and clearly identified as a simulation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, and exact. The interface should feel like a polished Android system utility with a restrained command layer. Its voice is factual and concise, especially around destructive actions and uncertain evidence.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a chat interface pasted onto a file manager, a generic cleaner app, or a neon AI dashboard. Avoid cyberpunk gradients, glass panels, animated orbs, robot mascots, fake scanning, giant storage charts, confetti, decorative AI badges, and claims that inaccessible or ambiguous files are safe to delete.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before recommendation: distinguish byte proof, user-directed scope, heuristics, and model assistance.
- Control before action: expose constraints and exact selected items before requesting deletion.
- Protection is visible and enforceable: show protected scopes and retained duplicate copies throughout review.
- Report demo outcomes honestly: distinguish fixture estimates and simulated removal; never claim measured device recovery or live AI inference from a bounded parser.
- Familiar utility behavior: use Android-inspired visual patterns with accessible web controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support at least 48 CSS-pixel touch targets, scalable text, screen-reader labels, keyboard navigation, browser Back, visible focus/selection and color-independent status. Maintain WCAG AA contrast, targeting 7:1 for body text where practical. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
